Product

One place for the plan, the model and the conversation.

No exports, no plugins, no files sent back and forth. It all runs in the browser.

Draw the floor plan

Drag rooms onto a grid, resize walls, add doors and stairs, or have a layout drafted from a bedroom and bathroom count.

See it in 3D, instantly

Every change rebuilds the house. Fly around it, walk through each floor at eye height, and move the sun to check the light.

Share with buyers

Send a link to view, pick finishes or change the plan. Comments are pinned to the exact spot in the house, and you approve what comes back.

How it works

From sketch to sign-off in three steps.

Start a design

Begin with a blank page, a sample home, or an auto layout, then shape it in the plan editor.

Review it in 3D

Check room sizes, sightlines and finishes from the outside in, floor by floor.

Send it out

Create a project in Studio and share a link. Feedback, changes and versions land in one place.

Open the playground and start drawing.

No sign-up needed to try it. Create a free account to save designs and share them with buyers.
