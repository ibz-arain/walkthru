Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This explains what Demo collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store your actual password.
  • Your work: designs, projects, versions, comments and the settings you choose.
  • Customer details you enter: the name and email of buyers you share a project with, so we can show and send them their link.
  • Usage and errors: pages visited, a few product events (for example “design created” or “link shared”) and error reports, so we can fix problems and improve the product. We skip product events when your browser sends Do Not Track.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. The only cookie we set keeps you signed in.

Designs in your browser

If you use the playground without an account, your designs are stored only in your own browser. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Who processes data for us

We use a hosting provider to run the site, a database provider to store data, and an email provider to send messages such as invites, password resets and notifications.

Emails

We send account emails (invites, password resets) and, if you leave notifications on, emails when customers comment or send changes. You can turn notifications off in Account settings.

Your choices

You can update your profile, change your password and leave workspaces from Account settings. To get a copy of your data or delete your account, contact us and we’ll handle it promptly.

Keeping data safe

Passwords are hashed, sessions expire, customer links can have passwords and expiry dates, and all traffic is encrypted in transit on the live site.
